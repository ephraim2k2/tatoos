Healing Protocol

Interactive Tattoo Aftercare Guide

Proper aftercare ensures maximum longevity, crisp line clarity, and vibrant tonal contrast for your tattoo.

Days 1 - 3: Protection & Sanitization

  • ✓
    Keep Bandage/SecondSkin Sealed: Leave protective film on for 24-48 hours as instructed by Daniel.
  • ✓
    Gentle Wash: Wash gently with lukewarm water and antibacterial unscented soap using clean palms only.
  • ✓
    Pat Dry: Air dry or pat gently with clean paper towels. Never rub with bath towels.

Days 4 - 14: Moisturization & Scab Prevention

  • ✓
    Apply Thin Lotion Layer: Apply a razor-thin layer of unscented ointment or lotion 2-3 times daily.
  • ✓
    Do NOT Scratch or Pick: Light flaking is normal. Never pick at scabs to preserve pigment intensity.
  • ✓
    Avoid Submersion: No swimming pools, hot tubs, or ocean water for at least 2 full weeks.

Month 1+: Lifetime UV Protection

  • ✓
    Apply SPF 50+ Sunscreen: Sun exposure is ink's greatest enemy. Apply high-SPF sunscreen when exposed to UV light.
  • ✓
    Free Touch-Up Guarantee: Every session includes a complimentary touch-up within 90 days of healing.
Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to book? ▼
Daniel's calendar typically opens 2-3 months in advance. We recommend booking early for weekend slots or multi-session sleeve projects.
Are deposits refundable or transferable? ▼
Deposits are non-refundable as they secure your custom design creation and studio time. However, you can reschedule your session without losing your deposit with at least 48 hours notice.
Can I request a custom design drawing before booking? ▼
Custom artwork stencils are created after your booking deposit is confirmed. You will review and refine the artwork directly with Daniel prior to tattooing.
Is numbing cream available during the session? ▼
Yes! We offer medical-grade topical numbing preparations for long sessions upon request during consultation.
